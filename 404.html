Tomax local currency stablecoin research

Tomax is a proposed collateral-backed system for tokens tracking Iranian rials, Turkish lira, and Argentine pesos. Most borrowing would require buying the issued token and locking it with an external asset in managed liquidity positions. A smaller branch would accept liquid volatile collateral such as ETH. The objective is to maintain currency pegs with modest market-making capital, permissionless settlement, and user-funded FX updates.

This is design research and a request for proposals. Production smart contracts, oracle infrastructure, legal analysis, audits, and operating capital are separate deliverables.

Principal result

The target trading range is 0.97 to 1.00 of external parity, starting at 1.00. In the baseline model, $10,000 of total seed LP value, 90% LP-backed additional debt, 10% ETH-backed additional debt, and 87.4% actual LTV do not produce a finite issuance ceiling at the 0.97 lower boundary. Proportional issuance adds liquidity and approaches price 0.970873, a 2.913% discount from parity. The result assumes continuing borrower capital, fixed FX, one common range, and no withdrawals or secondary selling.

Capital and scope

The working range is $10,000 to $50,000 of seed LP value per stablecoin, rather than a shared sum. Three simultaneous launches therefore require $30,000 to $150,000 of initial quote-asset LP value, plus liquidation capital, oracle operations, and security work. Starting at the upper boundary makes this seed position entirely external quote assets. The first loan can add quote-only LP collateral and then issue and sell debt. As borrowing grows, pool TVL increasingly includes the issued token and must not be confused with external reserves.

The token tracks a local currency and inherits its depreciation. It serves local denomination, payments, or borrowing; an IRR peg alone does not protect a holder from IRR inflation.

Design direction

  • Evaluate FiRM-style LP collateral as the closest production precedent for the main borrowing branch.
  • Compare borrower-selected interest against currency-specific borrowing rights before selecting the interest engine.
  • Keep market access, collateral eligibility, interest policy, redemptions, and liquidation funding as separate decisions.
  • Require atomic LP recovery and debt netting, externally authenticated FX reports, and explicit backstop funding.
  • Treat offchain hedges as optional management of already-funded inventory. Unexecuted hedges contribute no collateral value.

FiRM uses tokenized borrowing rights and has accepted LP collateral containing its own issued asset. Liquity provides permissionless redemptions and Stability Pool debt offset. These are relevant precedents, not evidence that a combined implementation has inherited their security. Production references.

Documents for review

Start with simulation, architecture, and security. Market making explains offchain policy versus onchain execution. The FiRM benchmark records the supplied $68.57m LP borrowing comparator and estimated $4.022m residual debt. Use the RFP to request independent economic design, an implementable specification, security validation, and chain-specific gas measurements. Accepted assumptions and unresolved choices are recorded in decisions.
