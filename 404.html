Tomax local currency stablecoin research

Tomax is a proposed collateral-backed system for tokens tracking Iranian rials, Turkish lira, and Argentine pesos. Most borrowing would require buying the issued token and locking it with an external asset in managed liquidity positions. A smaller branch would accept liquid volatile collateral such as ETH. The objective is to maintain currency pegs with modest market-making capital, permissionless settlement, and user-funded FX updates.

This is design research and a request for proposals. Production smart contracts, oracle infrastructure, legal analysis, audits, and operating capital are separate deliverables.

Latest FXSwap experiment

Replacing V3 with the inspected ZCHF/crvUSD FXSwap parameters gives a fixed-FX burst boundary of $31,605 additional debt per $10k of external quote seed, or $28,627 when the 0.97 marginal bid floor includes swap fees. At $50k external seed those figures are $158,025 and $143,136. FXSwap requires an equal-value own-token seed leg, recorded as a treasury liability: $10k external seed creates $20k initial LP TVL and $10k bootstrap issuance.

Those figures are launch-time entry-flow boundaries. In the main historical case, new debt starts at half the boundary and then remains fixed. The 179-day IRR replay fails the 3% peg target when arbitrage is restricted to real issued tokens outside the LP. Testing refuels from $0 to $100/day per $10k seed does not fix that constraint. A hypothetical unlimited outside-supply benchmark can track sampled spot more closely, but requires unmodeled additional token issuance and backing. The proposed above-peg debt-expansion mechanism is assessed as a separate, explicitly funded sensitivity.

The study uses the user-selected 75% opening ETH LTV, with 60%/67% sensitivity, and external seed capital as the main comparison while showing both seed interpretations. It includes six preselected calm/median/high-volatility days with 8,640 actual minute candles, source-based pool accounting, LP returns versus holding, treasury equity after bootstrap debt/refuels, ETH cohort exposure and separate gas assumptions. On the June 15 stress-day launch, the $10k-seed case underperforms holding the starting LP assets by about $710; treasury equity loses about $400, and LP borrowers' post-entry equity falls about $874. Earlier V3 comparison

The V3 model starts with quote-only seed at price 1.00 in a 0.97–1.00 band. Under fixed FX and continuing proportional LP entry, its 96% LP / 4% ETH debt path approaches price 0.972703 without reaching the lower edge. It nevertheless has finite secondary selling capacity at any point. A separate 20%-of-debt sale requirement gives approximately $108,394 additional debt per $10k seed. These results belong to that V3 construction, not FXSwap. V3 assumptions, generated tables.

FX operations and an alternate quote asset

A 179-day hourly-close backtest using Wallex IRR and Binance TRY/ARS data produced 100 aggregate resets at a 2% anchor threshold, or 37 with an asymmetric trigger intended to keep the entire band within 5% of parity. The 0.97 lower bound itself makes a 2% entire-band tolerance impossible. Missing IRR bars and intrahour spikes limit these workload estimates. Historical FX analysis.

At an assumed 600,000 gas per reset and 5 gwei, those policies imply average range fees of 0.001676 and 0.000620 ETH/day respectively across all three currencies. Liquidations, sponsorship, bridge fees and trading losses are separate. Daily ETH budget.

The local-currency partnership scenario pairs each Tomax token with a savings share backed by a stablecoin in the same currency: Zarban for IRR, and separate providers to be selected for TRY and ARS. Relative targets use each provider's savings ratio and denomination. Partner peg/exit risk, independent peg checks and ETH collateral pricing remain necessary; cross-chain risks depend on the selected provider's deployment.

Capital and scope

The working range is $10,000 to $50,000 of seed per stablecoin, with the denominator stated separately for each AMM. For the FXSwap external-seed interpretation, three launches require $30k–$150k external quote capital, create $60k–$300k initial LP TVL, and also create $30k–$150k bootstrap token liabilities. If $10k–$50k instead means total two-sided LP TVL per market, external quote capital is half that. Liquidation capital, refuels, oracle operations, and security are additional. Borrower-supplied capital and pool TVL containing self-issued tokens must not be confused with outside reserves.

The token tracks a local currency and inherits its depreciation. It serves local denomination, payments, or borrowing; an IRR peg alone does not protect a holder from IRR inflation.

Design direction

  • Evaluate FiRM-style LP collateral as the closest production precedent for the main borrowing branch.
  • Compare borrower-selected interest against currency-specific borrowing rights before selecting the interest engine.
  • Keep market access, collateral eligibility, interest policy, redemptions, and liquidation funding as separate decisions.
  • Require atomic LP recovery and debt netting, externally authenticated FX reports, and explicit backstop funding.
  • Treat offchain hedges as optional management of already-funded inventory. Unexecuted hedges contribute no collateral value.

FiRM uses tokenized borrowing rights and has accepted LP collateral containing its own issued asset. Liquity provides permissionless redemptions and Stability Pool debt offset. These are relevant precedents, not evidence that a combined implementation has inherited their security. Production references.
