Collateral onboarding requirements

Collateral expansion must preserve recoverable backing and liquidation capacity. A liquid ticker is insufficient if its accepted wrapper, chain, or redemption route cannot settle during stress.

Initial categories

Category Required valuation Main failure modes
Own token and external asset LP Recoverable per-position inventory, external FX, approved range and AMM Reflexive valuation, manipulated balances, one-sided inventory, unauthorized repositioning
ETH Independent ETH/USD and currency FX Price gaps, correlation, stalled keepers, insufficient debt-token liquidity
Yield-bearing external token Asset-market price, vault exchange rate, redeemability and delays Depeg, share inflation, cooldown, bridge or custodian exposure

ETH LTV is unresolved. The 87.4% actual LP LTV must not be copied to ETH. The 90/10 debt mixture fixes sale volume in the simulation but does not determine required ETH collateral or its liquidation safety.

Adapter admission

Each proposal must identify token contracts and implementations, admin powers, upgrades, transfers, fees, rebases, decimals, wrapper claims, dependencies, approved venues, and expected redemption delay. Include external executable liquidity across stressed notionals, keeper economics, correlated exposures, and oracle fallbacks.

For V3 collateral, specify factory and pool validation, position ownership, ticks and fee tier, in-range and out-of-range inventory, fee entitlements, manager authority, and an atomic recover-and-net path. Spot reserve composition is observable but remains manipulable within a transaction. Valuation must demonstrate that a flash trade, liquidity donation, or attacker-selected range cannot inflate borrowing power.

Admission requires tests for balance manipulation, supply/share donation, rounding, callback reentrancy, bad token behavior, stale FX, and quote-asset depeg. Test loss attribution when the same underlying pool backs many vaults.

Parameters and exposure controls

Configure collateral-specific borrow LTV, liquidation threshold, incentive, debt ceiling, rolling issuance limit, minimum economic vault size, concentration limit, oracle age, and accepted uncertainty. Borrow LTV and liquidation threshold are distinct parameters. Record whether caps are native currency or external value and how FX movements affect them.

Market headroom must reserve quote capacity for existing exits and queued settlement. A 10% target for volatile-backed debt is an upper policy envelope, not a right to borrow through exhausted quote reserves.

Rollout and retirement

Use review, a timelocked allowlist change, a small capped pilot, observed settlement, and measured increases. A guardian may reduce new exposure rapidly but must not grant arbitrary asset-transfer authority. Retirement stops new borrowing, preserves repayment, provides an exit path, and accounts for existing debt until settled. Adding or removing an asset never silently transfers its loss to another currency market.

Design research. No production contracts or return promises. Public documentation; noindex is not access control.
