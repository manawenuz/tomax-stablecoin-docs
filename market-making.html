Market making and range management

The intended target trading range is 0.97 to 1.00 of external currency parity, starting at 1.00. The upper boundary is parity. The arithmetic midpoint is 0.985, but it is not the starting price or a replacement currency benchmark. Debt expansion above parity is a proposed separate mechanism.

Starting at parity

For a position quoted as external assets per issued token, a V3 range ending at 1.00 holds entirely the external quote asset when price starts at 1.00. A $10,000 seed therefore begins with $10,000 of external quote and no issued-token principal in that position. The first LP borrower deposits quote only, locks the position, and can mint debt against it. Selling that debt moves the pool below parity, where subsequent positions require both assets.

The bootstrap must permit a zero stablecoin leg. Requiring an initial stablecoin purchase from this pool while it has no stablecoins would block the first loan. Actual pool token order, decimals, and tick rounding must be normalized before implementing this description. Outside holders and any seed issuance remain separate liabilities.

An existing V3 NFT's tick bounds are not modified in place. Repositioning removes liquidity from the old range and creates or increases another approved position. The NFT ownership and custody transition must remain atomic for locked collateral. The V3 position-manager interface exposes minting, liquidity changes, and collection.

V4 hooks can validate or perform custom behavior around swaps and liquidity changes. They do not make state changes happen offchain or without a triggering transaction. Hook execution consumes gas in the triggering transaction and introduces callback, accounting, and routing requirements. A custom oracle-priced curve is a new mechanism, not an automatic property of ordinary V4 liquidity. V4 hook documentation.

When to move the range

Let R be external quote-asset units per native currency token according to accepted FX and quote-asset prices. Target raw bounds are 0.97 R and 1.00 R, rounded according to the chosen pool. R changes as local FX moves; a yield-bearing quote also changes value. Neither V3 nor standard V4 ticks automatically follow R.

Ordinary swaps inside a still-valid range do not require moving it. Do not recenter merely because the issued token trades below parity: that could move liquidity away from the intended support policy. Use the external benchmark, permitted drift, valid reports, inventory limits, and post-action vault health.

No keeper is required for each individual swap in a plain pool, but a keeper transaction is required when range repositioning is needed. An authorized user transaction can carry a fresh report and bounded rebalance work. The smart contract validates the policy even if the offchain keeper is malicious.

The historical FX study distinguishes 2%/5% anchor drift from the deviation of the entire 0.97–1.00 band. Its close-only workload counts do not guarantee intrahour compliance or affordable inventory conversion. The daily ETH budget prices gas separately from slippage, losses and refill capital. A same-currency Zarban savings-share pool changes the target to a denomination-adjusted savings ratio; TRY/ARS would still require cross-FX.

Gas and security constraints

Batch report verification, collateral accounting, and bounded repositioning when measurements show a saving. Consider reviewed fungible wrappers or pooled management to avoid touching every borrower NFT per update, while preserving ownership and loss allocation. No unbounded loop over all vaults is permitted.

Use benchmark-driven thresholds rather than a mandatory reposition on every report. Set maximum drift, maximum report age, slippage bounds, target allowlists, recipient restrictions, and a post-rebalance health check. Batching does not eliminate adverse selection before a truthful FX update, and an oracle pause does not freeze an ordinary V3 pool.

Above parity policy

At the upper boundary the modeled LP has no issued tokens left to sell. The position alone cannot provide further stablecoin supply to buyers. A reviewed issuance or mint-and-sell route must define who provides backing, receives proceeds, pays fees, and satisfies debt and collateral limits. An automatic unbacked mint is not implied.

The current issuance simulation holds FX fixed and sells newly issued debt below the starting parity. It does not simulate the above-parity expansion mechanism or repeated moving ranges.
