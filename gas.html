Gas requirements

Gas efficiency must preserve correct valuation and recoverability during congestion. Optimize duplicated work and transaction structure before postponing unfunded risk.

The daily ETH budget combines observed FX-reset counts with explicitly assumed gas quantities. It separates one pooled position from ten positions per market, user-funded activity from treasury spending, and ordinary traffic from stressed settlement. These planning scenarios do not replace the measured deliverables below.

The FXSwap alternative moves its center inside eligible pool transactions, so V3 NFT range-reset counts do not carry over. Separately measure ordinary swaps versus swaps that update EMA, recenter, burn refuel shares, or reject a recenter, plus quote-only refuels, approvals and admin claims. The current 250,000-gas daily-refuel input is an assumption. Token funding for the unrecoverable refuel and ETH gas are separate costs.

Candidate optimizations

  • Accrue debt through reviewed aggregate indices rather than touching every vault.
  • Verify one report per market per transaction and reuse it across a bounded batch.
  • Batch independent debt burns and collateral-claim transfers where accounting remains deterministic.
  • Net recovered own tokens against the corresponding debt before requiring external token sourcing.
  • Avoid external asset swaps in the critical liquidation path when a funded backstop can accept collateral claims.
  • Use bounded storage layouts and numeric widths supported by explicit lifetime and overflow bounds.
  • Keep event data sufficient for offchain monitoring without requiring onchain iteration through all vaults.

Packing, unchecked arithmetic, custom signature aggregation, and cross-vault netting require proofs and measurements; none is a default requirement.

Benchmark deliverables

The deployment chain is undecided. Proposers must report exact gas and fees on their nominated chain and version, including calldata or L1 data costs on L2s. Report typical, maximum supported, and failure-path costs; do not present a dollar estimate without the gas-price and asset-price assumptions.

Each quote must identify reused dependencies, report byte size, signatures, storage accesses, external calls, execution cap, and keeper compensation. Set gas targets only after comparable chain-specific benchmarks exist.

Queue sponsorship

New borrowers can sponsor a capped amount of old settlement. Include this in the displayed borrowing cost before signing. A queue must progress without new borrowing, and a risk-reducing repayment must not trigger an unbounded or unrelated obligation. Moving work to a future borrower is not itself a gas saving.

Economic minimums

Minimum vault size, liquidation incentive, and bounty reserves must cover adverse gas conditions and actual collateral exit costs. Local currency depreciation can turn a once-economic vault into dust in dollar terms. Define who funds resolution and how native-unit minimums are updated without stranding existing users.
