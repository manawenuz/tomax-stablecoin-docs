Secondary selling envelopes

Maximum additional debt when the pool must still absorb the stated fraction of that debt in a separate sale, after issuance and without further LP, borrowing, or redemptions. The target trading range is 0.97 to 1.00 and the starting price is 1.00. Each sale ends at the 0.97 lower boundary. Include any other initial liabilities, liquidation demands, and a margin before using such a rule in risk policy.
