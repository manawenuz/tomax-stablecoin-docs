FX oracle requirements

The system uses externally authenticated FX data submitted on demand. A user paying submission gas does not establish the price's authenticity. Every price-sensitive action must verify the same market definition, source freshness, and report validity.

Benchmarks

IRR is intended to track an accessible composite market benchmark. Candidate inputs include executable USDT markets on multiple exchanges, Chand or other rate aggregators, and curated Telegram dealer quotes. Source weights are design parameters to be calibrated against historical data and manipulation costs, not fixed percentages selected without evidence.

Chand describes aggregating several sources. Several Telegram channels and an application may therefore represent one underlying dealer quote. Assign weight caps by independent source group and economic venue. A weighted median or another robust estimator must tolerate the loss or corruption of the maximum permitted group. Record input evidence and its original observation time for investigation.

Exchange inputs should use bid and ask depth at specified notionals, stale-book detection, spread filters, and venue health. Last trades and displayed top-of-book quotes alone are insufficient. Telegram inputs require authenticated channel identity, timestamp interpretation, bid/ask meaning, and treatment of edited, deleted, copied, or contradictory messages. Retain a consistent market definition when a source is missing.

Normalize rial and toman units explicitly. If q is local currency per USDT and u is USD per USDT, then USD per local currency is u/q. A USDT premium, withdrawal restriction, or depeg must not silently become an IRR valuation error.

TRY has a listed Chainlink feed on Ethereum. Deployment must confirm actual supported network, feed contract, liveness, heartbeat, deviations, and market hours. Do not assume a listed feed is suitable or that an equivalent feed exists for IRR or ARS. Each currency needs its own benchmark specification. Oracle sources.

Report verification

Reports must bind price, units and exponent, uncertainty, source observation time, aggregation time, expiry, monotonic sequence, market ID, chain ID, and verifying contract. Independent reporters must reproduce the source aggregation policy; many signatures from one data collector do not create independent observations.

Reject invalid signatures, replay across chains or markets, nonmonotonic reports, future timestamps outside tolerance, stale observations, insufficient source quorum, excessive dispersion, and excessive collateral/FX timestamp skew. Signing an old quote again must not refresh it. Small permitted report-age windows reduce exposure but do not prove that a selected signed report is the latest available report.

Atomic update and action

Anyone may submit a valid report and borrow, redeem, or settle a liquidation in the same transaction. Use a shared verified report for a bounded batch where safe. Freshness is measured in seconds from source observation and calibrated per chain and operation. A blanket three-block rule is not equivalent on different chains or sequencers.

Repayment and adding collateral should remain possible during ordinary price outages when they unambiguously reduce risk. Minting, collateral withdrawal, collateral-priced redemption, and liquidation require an accepted valuation or an explicitly reviewed fallback and settlement process. Token transfers themselves do not require oracle gating.

Outages and large moves

Do not clamp a genuine devaluation to an obsolete price and continue allowing value extraction. A circuit breaker should restrict risk-increasing actions and require additional verification or orderly settlement. Define market closures, weekend operation, network partitions, stale quotes with fresh API responses, and gaps on reopening.

A normal V3 pool remains tradable when vault operations are paused. V4 hooks can validate swaps or modify fees, but a custom hook adds code, gas, routing constraints, and exit behavior requiring review. Repositioning a V3 LP after an FX report does not recover losses from informed trades that occurred before the update.

Design research. No production contracts or return promises. Public documentation; noindex is not access control.
