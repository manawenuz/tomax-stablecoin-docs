Request for proposals

Project and objective

Design and validate collateral-backed IRR, TRY, and ARS tracking tokens with $10k to $50k seed LP value per currency. Most loans should require locked LP collateral containing the issued token and an external asset. A smaller reviewed branch may accept liquid volatile collateral such as ETH. Security, recoverability, and realistic gas costs are acceptance requirements.

This RFP requests research and specification proposals first. Production deployment requires a separately approved implementation, independent review, remediation, and operating plan. No implementation or audit engagement is commissioned by publication of this RFP.

Required baseline

  • Model 96% of additional debt backed by LP and 4% by ETH, with 87.4% actual LP LTV.
  • Start with $10k, $20k, $30k, $40k and $50k total seed LP value per currency, a 0.97 to 1.00 target trading range, and initial price 1.00 with quote-only seed liquidity.
  • Treat LP borrowing as buy, deposit, lock, mint, and sell, using actual AMM price impact and post-purchase inventory.
  • Permit quote-only LP entry at the starting upper boundary. Specify collateral-backed expansion above parity separately.
  • Compare the base case with the supplied FiRM LP-collateral benchmark using consistent gross debt, own-token collateral, external assets, and residual-debt denominators.
  • Separate seed liabilities, borrower collateral, external reserves, and liquidation backstop funding.
  • Support user-funded submission of authenticated FX reports with independent freshness and source checks.
  • Permit reviewed collateral adapters without giving core vaults arbitrary execution powers.
  • Evaluate deferred asset disposal and bounded borrower-sponsored queue processing.
  • Price security and operations separately from liquidity capital.
  • Replicate the historical FX reset workload, then extend it to higher-frequency multi-source prices, inventory conversion, slippage, outages and real keeper latency. Separate 2%/5% anchor tolerance from total-price tolerance.
  • Evaluate matching local-currency savings-share quote routes against direct FX pools: Zarban for IRR, with separate TRY and ARS providers to be selected. Include source receipt accounting, any required bridge and authenticated rate delivery, independent peg policy, and committed exits.

Required economic stress cases

Compare centered and imbalanced pools, common and heterogeneous ranges, realistic fee tiers and ticks, coarse and adversarial loan ordering, ETH-first bursts, borrower loops, genuine holder demand, concurrent exits, loan closure, LP withdrawal, and redemptions. Include local FX devaluation and appreciation gaps, source disagreement, quote-asset depeg, sUSDe-like share appreciation and cooldown, interest accumulation, ETH drawdowns, gas spikes, and failed offchain hedges.

Report both maximum issuance inside the band and the smaller amount supportable under specified exit and liquidation assumptions. Do not label a high gross-debt-to-seed ratio as capital efficiency without showing recoverable external backing and committed risk capital.

Proposal contents

Provide relevant team experience and publicly reviewable work, the exact upstream code and licenses considered, intended deliverables and file formats, milestone schedule, dependencies on the client, reviewer independence, itemized fees and exclusions, recurring operating costs, and a risk register. Identify which results require market data, a deployment chain, or custody and entity decisions.

Separate design, optional reference implementation, independent economic review, smart-contract audits, remediation, and launch support. Explain how competing code and interest choices change cost and security scope. Offer stop points after economic feasibility and after specification review.

Acceptance gates

  1. Independent replication of the baseline and an explanation of its lack of a finite issuance ceiling.
  2. Exit envelopes with complete liabilities and explicit funding assumptions; explain why 5%/10% exits give no finite cap in the ideal 96/4 model and impose independent risk constraints.
  3. A valuation and liquidation design meeting the security invariants under manipulation and outages.
  4. A bounded gas and keeper-economics plan for the nominated chain.
  5. Resolved critical choices and an implementable specification before production work is approved.
  6. Independent reviews and funded operational readiness before accepting uncapped user exposure.

Exclusions and rights

The current package contains no token sale, return promise, wallet integration, production contracts, audit certification, or commitment to any provider. Proposals must identify intellectual property and licensing terms, data rights, access requirements, and ongoing support obligations. Send proposals only through a channel designated by the project owner; no submission address is assumed here.

Design research. No production contracts or return promises. Public documentation; noindex is not access control.
